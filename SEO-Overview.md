# Prolper Website — SEO: What We Added & Why

*A plain-English summary of the search-engine optimization (SEO) work on the Prolper
website. Written for a general audience — no technical background needed.*

---

## What is SEO, in one line?

SEO is everything that helps Google (and other search engines) **understand what our
website is about** and **show it to the right people** — mainly folks in Mississauga
searching for the services we offer, like *"handyman near me"* or *"house cleaning
Mississauga."* Better SEO = we appear higher in search results = more customers find us.

We focused the SEO around **the services Prolper provides** and our **location
(Mississauga, Ontario).**

---

## What we added (and what each thing does)

### 1. Clear, keyword-rich page titles & descriptions
Every page now tells Google exactly what it's about, in the words customers search for.
- The homepage targets: *local services in Mississauga — handyman, cleaning, painting,
  car detailing, pet care, tutoring, CPA, fitness.*
- Each service has its **own page and its own title** (e.g. "Painting in Mississauga —
  Book a Local Pro"), so we can rank for each service individually.
- The "Become a Pro" page targets people who want to **work** with us.

**Why it matters:** the page title is the single biggest thing Google uses to rank and
to write the clickable blue headline you see in search results.

### 2. "Business card" data for Google (structured data)
We added hidden, machine-readable info that tells Google:
- **Who we are** — Prolper, our logo, our social media pages.
- **What we do** — a list of all our services, and that we serve **Mississauga, ON.**
- **Our app** — available on iOS and Android.
- **Our FAQs** (on the Become a Pro page) — which can show up directly in Google as
  expandable Q&A.

**Why it matters:** this can earn us richer, more eye-catching search listings and
helps Google trust and correctly categorize us.

### 3. A map of the site for search engines
- A **sitemap** — a list of every page we want Google to find (home, each service page,
  Become a Pro, legal pages).
- A **robots file** — instructions that welcome search engines and point them to the sitemap.

**Why it matters:** this helps Google discover and index all our pages faster.

### 4. Better link previews (social sharing)
When someone shares a Prolper link on WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn or X, it now shows a
proper **preview card** — our banner image, a title and a description — instead of a bare
link.

**Why it matters:** shared links look professional and get more clicks.

### 5. Location targeting
We tagged the site with our city (**Mississauga, Ontario**), so Google knows we're a
**local** service and can show us to nearby searchers.

### 6. Speed & mobile basics
- The site loads faster (we pre-connect to the services that supply images/fonts).
- It can be "installed" like an app on phones, uses our brand color, and works cleanly
  on mobile.

**Why it matters:** Google ranks fast, mobile-friendly sites higher.

---

## The result

- The homepage and **every service** are now individually optimized to be found on
  Google for local searches in Mississauga.
- Our listings can appear richer (FAQs, business info).
- Shared links look polished everywhere.
- Search engines can find and understand all our pages.

---

## What's still needed from us (to get the full benefit)

1. **Confirm the website address.** The SEO is set up for **www.prolper.com**. If the
   final address is different, we update it in one place before going live.
2. **Submit the site to Google.** After the site is live, we register it in **Google
   Search Console** and submit the sitemap so Google starts indexing us. (Free.)
3. **Create a Google Business Profile.** For a local service in Mississauga, this is one
   of the biggest wins — it's what puts us on Google Maps and in the "local pack" for
   *"near me"* searches. (Free.)
*(Note: "pre-rendering" — making every page perfect for all search engines and social
platforms, not just Google — is now **done**. See the update below.)*

---

## Update — stronger SEO (round 2)

Two big upgrades were added on top of the above:

1. **Every page is now "pre-rendered."** Previously, only Google could fully read our
   app-style site; now every page is saved as a complete web page, so **all** search
   engines *and* social platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, X) read the full
   content and show the right preview. This is one of the biggest possible technical
   SEO wins.

2. **Each service now has a real, detailed page.** Every service (handyman, cleaning,
   painting, car detailing, pet care, CPA, tutoring, fitness) has its own page with:
   - a unique, keyword-rich description focused on **Mississauga**,
   - a "what you can book" list,
   - a **FAQ section** (which can appear as expandable Q&A directly in Google),
   - links to our other services.

   Thin, near-empty pages don't rank — these now have real substance to rank on.

*(One process note: to publish these, the site is built with the command
`npm run build:prod`, which generates the pre-rendered pages. Details are in the
technical SEO doc.)*

The website now speaks Google's language, centered on **our services** and **our city**.
